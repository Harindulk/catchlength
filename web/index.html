        About the build

        Built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, Indie track. All code written from 27 Oct 2026.

        What it does

        Sri Lanka already measures coastal tuna from photographs, but a person still draws a line on every fish and names every species by hand. CatchLength does that work from a 20-second phone clip of fish laid next to printed marker sheets, and it can't skip checking its own work.

        Theme: Build for Your World. "Bring Meta's models into your domain. Solve a problem you know firsthand, free up time and mental space, and make something possible that wasn't before."

        How it checks itself

        The checks live inside the agent's tools, so the model can't skip them. Calibration refuses a bent or missing sheet. A count that disagrees with the plan sends Muse Spark's boxes back to SAM 3.1 with negative points on the neighbouring fish (tested on scripted runs; on the real models SAM 3.1 separated touching fish on its own, so this never had to fire). Every fish is measured two ways and in several frames; when they disagree it is flagged, never averaged. A tool refused three times hands the job to the officer, who signs off on the report. The tape measurements it is graded on are never shown to it.

        Rules it cites

        No minimum landing size for the tunas and mackerel sampled here was found in the gazetted regulations we reviewed, so the report says "no rule found" for each of them. It cites the lobster and mud crab rules where they apply, and IOTC's rule that size data must be binned at 5 cm or finer.

        Meta models and what each one does

        Muse Spark 1.3
        Runs the agent loop (one tool per turn), plans the clip, finds missed and merged fish, places snout and fork points, names the species from a closed list.
        SAM 3.1
        Segments every fish from the concept "fish", then again from Spark's boxes and negative points when a fish was missed or two were merged.
        Muse Wonder 1.0
        Built, not yet run: a last look at the filed report. It can only add fish to the officer's queue, never change a length, a species or a status.
        Muse Spark Open
        Built, not yet run: the same bounded check by the open-weights model on Baseten, as an independent auditor.
